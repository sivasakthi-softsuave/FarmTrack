Diary: five design proposals proposal, nothing changed in the app

Today's Diary is a plain list: a flask, the medicine, the crop and a green tick. Everything below is built from what the Diary already has (the medicine, its dose and its waiting days, the crop, the date, warnings, saved or waiting), so nothing needs the server to change. The header, the red mute button, the bottom bar, the green "Write a spray" button, the grey "Waiting" pill, the green tick and "touch a card to hear it" stay in every option. The medicines, doses and waiting days in the pictures are examples. Choose one (or mix: for example 2 on top of 3) and I will build it.

The three answers of "Can I pick?" (option 2)

The same card, in the three states it can have. Amber means "wait" (it is a warning about eating or selling too early), green means "safe", grey means "I do not know" (a medicine the app does not have in its list has no waiting days, and the app never says "safe" without them).
