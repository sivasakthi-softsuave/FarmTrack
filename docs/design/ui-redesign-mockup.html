FarmTrack: new look (mockup)

A proposal for the Flutter app, shown on 12 screens at real phone size (390 × 844). The content inside each phone scrolls. Nothing in the app has changed yet: after you approve, the same design goes into app_theme.dart and the screens. Medicine, dose and warnings on the advice screen are the app's own demo data (HP 2026 schedule); names and places are the demo farmer F001.

Calmer, lighter screensWhite cards with soft shadows instead of thick coloured borders; a light green-grey ground; more room between things.
Right-sizedText 15–26 px instead of 20–36 px. Main buttons stay large (60–68 px); nothing is smaller than 48 px.
Clear structureBottom bar for Home, Field, Scan, Diary, Ask. Scan is the big green button in the middle, on every main page.
Voice stays firstThe speaker button is on every screen; the spoken sentence floats as a dark caption. Green tick, red cross and the pictures the voice names are kept.
Same colour meaningsGreen = yes / done, red = stop / danger, amber = warning only, blue = information, grey = waiting.
Hindi readyNoto Sans with Noto Sans Devanagari, so Hindi and English look the same weight and size.
